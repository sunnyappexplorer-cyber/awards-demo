Overview

The Western India Business Awards spotlight the companies, founders and institutions shaping the economy of Maharashtra, Gujarat and Goa. Now in its second year, the programme covers sectors from manufacturing and fintech to healthcare, education and the creative industries.

Every entry is reviewed by an independent panel of industry leaders, academics and policy experts. Winners are honoured at a black-tie ceremony in Pune and featured across our print, digital and video channels, giving their work the visibility it deserves.

How Our Awards Work

Judging Criteria

Entries are scored on measurable impact, innovation, scalability and community value.

Each criterion carries a published weight so every applicant knows the rules up front.

View weightage

Fair & Independent

Jury members declare conflicts of interest and step away from any category where they have a stake. Scores are averaged across at least three judges per entry.

Confidentiality

Financials and internal documents are seen only by the jury. Only your company name and a short citation are published if you are shortlisted or win.

Independent Audit

  • Eligibility check: every entry is verified by an external audit partner before judging.
  • Tamper-proof scoring: results are tabulated and sealed until the awards night.

Two-Stage Review

  • Sector experts score each entry on the published criteria.
  • The grand jury debates the top-ranked entries in a closed session.
  • Three finalists per category go through to the final round.

FAQs

What are the Western India Business Awards?

An annual programme recognising standout organisations and leaders across Maharashtra, Gujarat and Goa.

Why should my company enter?

Winning brings independent validation, media coverage and a year of bragging rights with clients and talent.

Who is eligible?

Any registered business, startup, NGO or institution with operations in the western region during the last 18 months.

How does the application and judging process work?

Submit the online form with supporting documents. Entries are audited, scored by sector experts, then reviewed by the grand jury.

What do winners receive?

A trophy, a digital winner's badge, a feature in our awards supplement and a seat at the leaders' roundtable.
